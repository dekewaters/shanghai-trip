Good to know

  • Load this page on your eSIM or roaming data. Some local wifi in China blocks overseas sites, so switch wifi off if it won't load.
  • Amap buttons open the place in the Amap app. Set Amap to English before you fly. For reviews, use Dianping inside WeChat (Discover → Mini Programs → Dianping, then ⋯ → translate).
  • Pay with Alipay. DiDi rides are inside Alipay too. Carry about ¥500 cash as backup.
  • Leaving the arena: walk 10-15 min away before calling a DiDi, or take Metro Line 8.
  • Ignore friendly strangers near Nanjing Rd and the Bund inviting you to tea or a bar.
  • Carry tissues. Mall bathrooms on the 2nd floor and up are the cleanest.
  • No tipping. Keep a photo of your passport on you.
  • Jet lag: daylight every day, caffeine before 1pm, naps under 20 min.
